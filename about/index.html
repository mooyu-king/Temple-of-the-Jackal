About Us

About this browser portal

Temple of the Jackal is an independent, unofficial browser portal built to make the supplied Godot game package playable in a modern web browser. The site presents the game, explains its color-matching rotation puzzles, and provides practical information about controls, browser compatibility, mature content, and progress tracking.

Who the site is for

This website and the embedded game are intended only for adults aged 18 or older. The surrounding page avoids explicit imagery, but the game can unlock mature story scenes containing nudity and sexual content. Visitors who are under 18 or who do not want to encounter such material should not launch the game.

Ownership and editorial independence

This portal is not the official website of the original developer or publisher. Game names, artwork, code, characters, and related assets remain the property of their respective owners. The website interface, gameplay guide, troubleshooting notes, and editorial descriptions were created specifically for this browser edition.

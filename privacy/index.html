Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026.

Information this site collects

You do not need to create an account or submit personal information to play Temple of the Jackal. The website does not ask for your name, address, payment details, or game profile. A hosting provider may process standard technical information such as an IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps, and error logs to deliver and protect the site.

Browser storage and game progress

The game and the optional Chamber Progress checklist may store progress on your device using browser-managed storage. Checklist data consists only of the chamber numbers you mark as complete. It remains in your browser and is not transmitted to this site. You can remove it with the Reset Progress button or by clearing site data in your browser.

Embedded YouTube videos

This site embeds videos hosted by YouTube. When you load or play an embedded video, Google or YouTube may receive technical data and use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies. You can choose not to play the videos.

Cookies, advertising, and third parties

This site currently uses third-party popunder advertising. The advertising script is included near the end of the page HTML and does not wait for client-side initialization, a timed delay, or scrolling. Advertising is not loaded on the About Us and Contact pages. An interaction may open an advertisement in a separate tab or window. Advertising providers may process IP addresses, browser and device information, referral data, ad interactions, cookies, or similar identifiers to deliver, measure, and limit advertising. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies.

Website analytics are delivered through data.1back.link. The analytics service may receive page URLs, referral information, browser and device information, IP addresses, and interaction events to help measure site usage and technical performance.

Data choices and contact

You can block browser storage, clear stored progress, decline embedded media, or stop using the site at any time. For privacy questions or requests, email mooyuking@gmail.com.

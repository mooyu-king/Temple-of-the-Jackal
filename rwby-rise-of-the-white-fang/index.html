Restricted file / 01

The archive begins where a hard-won peace stops feeling reliable

RWBY Rise of the White Fang opens after Salem has been defeated and Team RWBY has helped secure a fragile peace. The new danger does not erase that victory; it uses the desire to protect it. A wider power struggle, a familiar threat, and a White Fang plot place public stability beside private pressure. CorrhenGames describes the work as a plot-heavy corruption eroge, so the central record is not a combat score. It is the changing relationship between knowledge, loyalty, agency, and the boundaries each heroine can still defend.

This Temple entry hosts an independent browser conversion of the supplied public PC version 0.4. CorrhenGames' itch.io page remains the authoritative source for official Windows, macOS, and Android downloads, development posts, and later releases. The Ren'Py engine and scripts arrive first, while a media library containing hundreds of images and 188 WebM files is requested as the story reaches it. A new scene can therefore pause on its first opening even when the page and engine are functioning correctly.

The public images displayed outside the player are non-explicit. The embedded visual novel is restricted to adults and includes sexual content, corruption, mind-control, coercion, and other mature material. Confirming the notice delays the iframe; it is not identity verification and does not decide whether the material is lawful or comfortable for a visitor. Enter only in an appropriate private setting, preserve the ability to stop, and do not expose another person to the game without their consent.

Browser preservation / 06

Open the large Ren'Py record without sacrificing saves

After confirming the adult notice, keep the player tab visible while Ren'Py initializes. Click inside once if audio or keyboard controls remain inactive. A current desktop browser with JavaScript, WebAssembly, and IndexedDB enabled offers the most reliable first session. The protected host accepts approved parent sites and gives the player short-lived signed asset paths; opening the game subdomain directly returns a guide page instead of a playable session.

Hundreds of PNG images and 188 WebM files are delivered when requested. Close other memory-heavy tabs, avoid opening duplicate game sessions, and wait when a new animated scene appears for the first time. A phone can suspend a background tab or discard it under memory pressure, so save before switching apps and use landscape orientation. Mobile availability does not guarantee comfortable performance on every device.

Ren'Py browser saves belong to the game host's local storage. Use several named slots and Export Saves when available. Changing browsers, profiles, or parent sites can isolate progress, while private-mode cleanup and cleared site data can remove it. If a scene fails, note version 0.4, the operating system, browser, chapter, final visible line, and whether sound continued before reloading once. Do not erase the storage that may contain both your progress and the evidence needed to reproduce the problem.

Intake record / 01

The most dangerous explanation may be the one that makes the routine easiest to continue

Over Dose begins with pills, water, and repetition. The protagonist wakes into a pattern that appears manageable until room details, status values, and Evren make it difficult to tell whether care is preserving stability or defining what the protagonist is allowed to know.

This Temple archive reads the demo as a damaged case file rather than a completed diagnosis. The playable frame, five local images, two supplied videos, route analysis, browser notes, ten FAQs, and unofficial ending reflection keep verified evidence separate from speculation.

Browser procedure / 06

Give the remote visual novel time, focus, and storage

Press the launch button, allow the remote files to load, and click inside the frame before testing dialogue, room controls, sound, or timed input. Desktop fullscreen is recommended because small status text and object prompts matter.

If the player remains blank, refresh once and check extensions or networks that block scripts, storage, media, or iframes. Save behavior may depend on browser storage; private mode, profile changes, or clearing site data can remove progress.

Questions · 07

Over Dose FAQ

Useful answers with minimal spoilers and clear browser guidance.

01What is Over Dose?+

Over Dose is a psychological visual novel demo about medication, Anxiety, Awareness, room exploration, aftercare, and a complicated relationship with the doctor Evren.

02Can I play Over Dose online?+

Yes. Launch the embedded browser build above without installing a separate application.

03Who is Evren?+

The supplied chart describes Evren as a 26-year-old doctor whose connection to the protagonist feels unusually familiar.

04What do Anxiety and Awareness do?+

They provide different kinds of route feedback; one reflects pressure while the other reflects recognition, and neither is a complete morality score.

05Can I explore rooms?+

Yes. The screenshots show room movement prompts, clickable objects, an exit option, and an interface for carrying or using items.

06What is Aftercare?+

Aftercare is a quieter route option associated with recovery and reflection after an intense scene.

07What is Cafe Rush?+

It is a timed task where the player serves one customer at a time and responds to visible drink requests.

08Is the ending result official?+

No. This page provides an unofficial route tendency based only on your quiz answers and does not claim to reveal confirmed endings.

09Does it work on mobile?+

It may load on a current mobile browser, but desktop fullscreen provides clearer text and more reliable timed input.

10Why is the frame blank?+

Wait for assets, click the frame, refresh once, and check extensions or network filters.

11Is Over Dose suitable for children?+

Review it first; the demo includes medication, distress, anxiety, dependency, possible manipulation, blood marks, and an uneasy doctor-patient dynamic.

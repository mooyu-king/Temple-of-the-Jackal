Before your first run

Approach an encounter as a compact puzzle. Notice the initial state, decide on one move and look closely at the result before changing your plan.

A practical first attempt

Treat your first attempt as reconnaissance. Pick a landmark before a suspicious stretch of trail. After a surprise, change where or when you approach that landmark rather than rushing the entire path differently.

What to watch on a first run

The joke in a trap game depends on what you expect to happen. Before trying to dodge everything in sight, take the simple route once. Watch where the obstacle comes from as well as where you end up. The spot where a character falls and the spot that set the trap off are not necessarily the same.

A controlled second attempt

On a retry, use one stationary feature as your marker. Stop just short of it, then approach from a different distance or angle. Changing every part of the approach at once can get you past the obstacle without teaching you how to repeat it. Aim to reproduce one safe section rather than memorising the entire forest.

After the opening scene

The costume screen is separate from the forest. If you simply want to test the controls, pick the default figure instead of spending your first run in the designer. Save the video for after the first few surprises: seeing exactly where a trap fires can spoil more of this particular game than learning its controls.

Questions before playing

What kind of game is this?

A bright path leads into a forest that treats walking forward as an invitation to play a trick.

What should I try first?

Treat your first attempt as reconnaissance. Pick a landmark before a suspicious stretch of trail. After a surprise, change where or when you approach that landmark rather than rushing the entire path differently.

What does the opening screen show?

The opening screen identifies this build as a demo. Its setup lets you play the default figure or design a victim before entering the forest.

Can I see the guide without starting the game?

Yes. The player only opens after you select Play. The illustrated notes below it do not require a game download.

Does refreshing restart the player?

Refreshing unloads the current frame. Do not use it as an undo button; use the game's own menus and save options where offered.

Why is there a loading screen after I press Play?

The cover belongs to this page; the game downloads its own resources after launch. Wait for its menu before pressing keys repeatedly.

How can I avoid ending spoilers?

Read the opening notes and try a run yourself. Leave the optional demo walkthrough closed until you have encountered the forest traps yourself.
